# Abide privacy policy

**Effective date:** November 5, 2026

Abide is a free app that helps church leaders build a daily Bible reading habit together. It is made by Emman Roy Pielago ("we"). This policy explains what Abide collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We've kept it short and plain.

Abide has no ads, sells nothing, and never sells or rents your information.

## What we collect

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **Your name and email address** | To create your account, sign you in, and show your name to your overseer and friends. |
| **An account ID and a friend code** | To identify your account and let friends add you. Your friend code is only shared if you give it to someone. |
| **Your role and small groups** | To connect you with your overseer and your cell group or ministry (you join with the group's code). |
| **Check-ins** | The date you read and the Bible passage (e.g. "John 3"), and which reading plan day it was for, if any. Used to show your streak and progress. |
| **Your Read, Reflect, Respond notes** | The notes you type, or the text you review and keep after scanning your notebook. Saved so you can look back on them. |
| **Highlights** | The verse reference (not the Bible text), the colour you chose, and any note you add. |
| **Reading plan progress** | Which plan you're on and how far you've got. |
| **Friends and encouragements** | Who you're friends with, and the short ready-made encouragements you send ("Praying for you", etc.). There is no free-text messaging. |
| **Reminder time** | The time you choose for your daily reading reminder, so the app can remind you. |
| **A notification token** | A code from your phone that lets Abide send you a notification when a friend encourages you. It isn't your phone number and can't be used to identify you outside Abide. |
| **Problem reports** | What you write when you tap "Report a problem", with your name, so we can follow up. |
| **App diagnostics from Google ML Kit** | See "Notebook scan" below. |

We don't collect your location, contacts, phone number, payment details, or files.

## Notebook scan: your photos stay on your phone

When you scan a notebook page, the photo is read **on your phone** using Google ML Kit text recognition. The photo is **never uploaded and never saved**: it is deleted from the app as soon as the text is read. Only the text you review and choose to keep is saved, as your Read, Reflect, Respond notes.

Google ML Kit may send Google basic information about the device and app (such as device model, app version, a per-install identifier and performance data) to keep the feature working well. It doesn't send your photos or the recognised text. See [Google's ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

## Bible text

Bible text comes from the YouVersion Platform. When you open a chapter, Abide asks for that Bible version and chapter through our server. **No personal information is sent to YouVersion.** Bible text is shown on screen only and is never saved by Abide. Only verse references (like `JHN.3.16`) are stored for your highlights and check-ins.

## Who can see your information

- **You** can see everything you've saved.
- **Your overseer** (the leader of a group you joined) can see your name, whether you read today, your streak, and the passages you read. **Your overseer never sees your Read, Reflect, Respond notes or your highlights.**
- **Your friends** (only people you both agreed to add) see whether you read today and your streak. Never your passages or notes.
- **Abide admins** (a few people in the church who run the app) can see names, email addresses, roles, groups, check-in dates and problem reports, so they can help people and fix mistakes. They don't see your notes through the app.
- **Service providers** that run Abide for us (below). They handle data only to provide their service.

We never share your information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## Service providers

- **Supabase** stores the app's data and handles sign-in. Its servers are in Japan (Tokyo). Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
- **Google** provides ML Kit (on-device text recognition) and distributes the app through Google Play.
- **Expo** builds the app and, with **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging**, delivers notifications when a friend encourages you. They receive your notification token and the short message (e.g. "Ana: 🙏 Praying for you"). Daily reading reminders are created on your phone and don't go through any server.
- **YouVersion** provides Bible text, and receives no personal information (see above).

## How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile and everything linked to it are deleted straight away: check-ins, notes, highlights, plan progress, friendships, encouragements, group memberships and problem reports. Copies may remain for a short time in our hosting provider's backups and logs until they are replaced, after which they are gone too.

If you lead a group as an overseer, an admin first moves your groups to another overseer, so the people in them don't lose their group.

## Deleting your account

- **In the app:** Profile → **Delete my account**.
- **Without the app:** follow the steps at https://mannigoy.github.io/Abide/delete-account.html, or email emmanroy.pielago03@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We'll delete your account and data within 30 days.

## Keeping your data safe

All data is sent over encrypted connections. Each person can only reach their own data, plus what's described in "Who can see your information" (enforced by the database itself, not just the app). No passwords or keys for our servers are stored in the app.

## Age

Abide is made for church leaders and isn't intended for children under 13.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
