## Full description (see count at the bottom)

```
Abide helps church leaders build a daily Bible reading habit, together.

Each day you read your Bible and write in your own paper notebook using RRR: Read, Reflect, Respond. Then you check in on Abide. Your overseer can see who has read today, and your friends can cheer you on.

MADE FOR SMALL GROUPS
Abide is made for church leaders in cell groups and ministries. Anyone can create an account, and you join your small group with the code your overseer gives you.

TWO WAYS TO CHECK IN
• Simple: say you've read, and add the passage if you like.
• Guided: step through Read, Reflect and Respond and save your notes.
• Check in for today or yesterday, once a day.

SCAN YOUR NOTEBOOK
Wrote your RRR by hand? Take a photo of the page and Abide reads your handwriting on your phone. You review the text before saving. The photo never leaves your phone and is never saved.

READ THE BIBLE IN THE APP
Read any chapter in a range of Bible versions, provided by YouVersion. Highlight verses in soft colours and add a note. The copyright line for each version is shown with the text.

READING PLANS
Follow a devotional reading plan one day at a time. Devotional plans by NewSpring Church, used with credit.

STREAKS WITH GRACE
See your reading streak grow. Life happens, so each week includes one grace day.

FRIENDS AND ENCOURAGEMENT
Add friends with their friend code. Friends see only whether you've read today and your streak, never your notes. Send a kind word like "Praying for you" or "Keep going!"

FOR OVERSEERS
See which of your leaders have read today, their streaks and recent passages, so you know who to encourage. Overseers never see anyone's personal notes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your Read, Reflect, Respond notes are yours. Only you can see them.
• Notebook photos are read on your phone and never uploaded.
• No ads. Free.
• Delete your account any time from Profile.

Abide is a free, non-commercial app made for church leaders in Cebu, Philippines.
```
